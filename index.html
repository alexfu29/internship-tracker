Email drafts

Your own wording, kept here so you never leave the app to find it. Tap Copy draft anywhere in the tracker and the filled-in text goes to your clipboard — paste it into your mail app, tweak it, send it. Nothing is ever sent for you.

Placeholders get replaced automatically: {first} {name} {company} {role} {date} {days} {channel}. Anything with no value on file comes through as [company] so you spot the gap instead of sending a blank.

Backup without a token:

The token is stored only on this device (localStorage). It is never sent anywhere except directly to api.github.com.
